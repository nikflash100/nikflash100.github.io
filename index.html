Projects

Buzzer Station

Quizbowl practice that feels like a real match.

I wanted to practice tossups by myself, so I built an app that reads questions out word by word and lets you buzz with the spacebar. It has power markers, auto-grading, scoring by category, recap graphs, and a mode that drills only the questions you missed.

The hardest part was the parser. I pulled 1,885 tossups and 1,646 bonuses from public middle school packets, and every packet is formatted a little differently.

HTML, CSS, JavaScript
